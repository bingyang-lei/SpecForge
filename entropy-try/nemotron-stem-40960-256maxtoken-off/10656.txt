The correct answer is: **J: C-band experiences stronger canopy attenuation reducing soil contributions as biomass increases, while X-band primarily senses the top canopy layer consistently**

### Explanation:

The key to understanding this question lies in the **difference in how C-band and X-band SAR systems interact with vegetation** during the crop growth cycle.

- **C-band (L-band)** has **longer wavelength** and **greater penetration depth** into the vegetation canopy and soil. This means it can **penetrate deeper into the canopy and soil**, allowing it to **sense soil moisture and biomass changes** more effectively. As the crop matures and biomass increases, the **canopy becomes denser**, leading to **greater attenuation** of the C-band signal. This **reduces the contribution from the soil** and increases the **dominance of canopy scattering**.

- **X-band (K-band)** has **shorter wavelength** and **less penetration depth**. It is more sensitive to the **top layer of the canopy**, such as the **leaves and stems**, and is less affected by the **underlying soil**.
