[3] ASSISTANT
The correct answer is: **A: Oxygen tension increases progressively from periportal to pericentral hepatocytes, favoring oxidative metabolism in zone 3**

---

### Explanation:

In the **classic hexagonal hepatic lobule model**, the liver is organized in a **central-to-periportal** gradient, with the **portal triads** (portal vein, hepatic artery, and bile duct) located at the **periportal region**, and the **central vein** at the **center** of the lobule.

#### Key Features of the Lobule:

- **Blood flow**: Blood enters via the **portal vein** and **hepatic artery**, flows through **sinusoids**, and exits via the **central vein**.
- **Bile flow**: Bile is secreted from **canaliculi** into the **bile ducts**, which are located near the **portal triads**.
- **Metabolic zonation**:
  - **Zone 1**: Periportal (high oxygen, high nutrients, low metabolic activity)
  - **Zone 2**: Central (moderate oxygen, moderate metabolism)
  - **Zone 3**: Pericentral (low oxygen, high metabolic activity, especially oxidative metabolism)
