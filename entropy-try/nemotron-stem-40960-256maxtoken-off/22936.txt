[3] ASSISTANT
The correct answer is: **C: Construction of the oxetane ring via intramolecular epoxide opening**

### Explanation:

In the **total synthesis of paclitaxel (Taxol)**, **Robert Holton** and his team developed a **semisynthetic approach** starting from **10-deacetylbaccatin III**, a natural product derived from the Pacific yew tree. This approach was a major breakthrough in the synthesis of Taxol.

One of the **key synthetic challenges** in the total synthesis of Taxol was the **construction of the oxetane ring** (a four-membered ring containing one oxygen atom), which is a critical structural feature of the taxane core.

In Holton's semisynthetic approach, the **oxetane ring** was formed through an **intramolecular epoxide opening**. This reaction is **diastereoselective** and **stereocontrolled**, allowing the formation of the correct stereochemistry at the ring junction.
