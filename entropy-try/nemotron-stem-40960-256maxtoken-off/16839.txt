### **What are intrinsic surface states?**

Intrinsic surface states are electronic states that **exist within the bulk band gap** of a semiconductor at the surface. These states are **not due to adsorbates or impurities**, but are **intrinsic to the surface structure** of the crystal.

They arise due to **breaking of translational symmetry** at the surface, which can lead to the formation of **localized states** within the band gap.

---

- **Breaking of translational symmetry** at the surface is essential. This can occur due to **surface termination**, **surface reconstruction**, or **surface geometry**.
