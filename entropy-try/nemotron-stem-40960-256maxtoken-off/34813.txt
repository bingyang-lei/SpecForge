### **Key Concepts:**

- A **quarter-wave transformer** is a transmission line of length **λ/4** (one-quarter of a wavelength) that is used to match impedances.
- The **characteristic impedance** $ Z_{\text{transformer}} $ of the quarter-wave transformer is chosen such that:
  $$
  Z_{\text{transformer}} = \sqrt{Z_0 \cdot Z_L}
  $$
  where:
  - $ Z_0 $ is the impedance of the transmission line (here, 50 Ω),
  - $ Z_L $ is the load impedance (here, 100 Ω).

- This formula gives the **optimal impedance** for the quarter-wave transformer to achieve **maximum power transfer** at the **design frequency**.
