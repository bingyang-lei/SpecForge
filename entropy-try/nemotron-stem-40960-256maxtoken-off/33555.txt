To determine the **Mandel Q parameter** for photon counting statistics in the case of a **two-level atom** driven by a **resonant laser** under **continuous monitoring**, we need to analyze the **quantum state of the atom** and the **photon statistics** it emits.

---

### **Key Concepts:**

- **Two-level atom**: The atom has two energy levels, say ground and excited.
- **Rabi frequency Ω**: The strength of the driving laser.
- **Spontaneous emission rate γ**: The rate at which the atom emits photons.
- **Strong driving limit**: Ω >> γ, so the atom is mostly in the excited state.
- **Continuous monitoring**: This implies **quantum measurement** of the emitted photons, leading to **photon counting statistics**.
- **Mandel Q parameter**: Defined as:

  $$
  Q = \frac{\langle \Delta n^2 \rangle - \langle n \rangle}{\langle n \rangle}
  $$
